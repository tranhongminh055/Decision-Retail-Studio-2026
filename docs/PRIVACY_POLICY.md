## 1. Overview & Core Commitment
**Vibio Technology** ("we", "our", or "us") respects the privacy and confidentiality of the users of **DSS Retail Studio 2026**.

DSS Retail Studio 2026 is an offline-first desktop Decision Support System (DSS) utilizing Analytic Hierarchy Process (Saaty AHP) and mathematical optimization (Knapsack Solver) for retail sales and procurement planning.

> **Key Privacy Commitment:**  
> DSS Retail Studio 2026 does **NOT** collect, harvest, transmit, share, or sell any personal identifiable information (PII) or proprietary commercial data to external cloud servers. All operational data remains strictly stored on the user's local workstation.
