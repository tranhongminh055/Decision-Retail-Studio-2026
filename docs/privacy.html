1. Overview & Commitment

Vibio Technology ("we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy and security of users of DSS Retail Studio 2026 ("the Application").

DSS Retail Studio 2026 is an enterprise-grade Decision Support System engineered for retail and sales strategy analysis. It incorporates analytic hierarchy process (Saaty AHP) and mathematical optimization (Knapsack Solver) to help retailers make informed procurement, inventory allocation, and marketing budget decisions.

Key Commitment: DSS Retail Studio 2026 is architected as an offline-first desktop application. We do not collect, track, harvest, sell, or transmit your business data or personal identity information to any external server or third party.

🛡️

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

When using DSS Retail Studio 2026, we do not collect or process:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, phone numbers, home addresses, national IDs, or personal contact lists.
  • Financial Information: We do not collect bank accounts, credit card credentials, or payment transaction details.
  • Biometric Data: No facial recognition, fingerprint, or biometric identifiers are captured or processed.
  • Location Data: We do not track or record your device's geographical GPS location or network positioning.
  • Hardware & Device Identifiers: We do not gather unique device IDs, MAC addresses, or advertising identifiers.
💾

3. Local Data Storage & Ownership

All computational algorithms, retail datasets, product catalogs, customer profiles, criteria matrix evaluations, and sales forecasts remain exclusively on the user’s local workstation:

  • Local Database: Data is persisted in a local database (SQLite / Local SQL Server instance) residing in the application's isolated local app data directory.
  • User Ownership: You retain 100% legal ownership and control over all retail data entered into the software.
  • Zero Cloud Sync: No data synchronization or background cloud backup to external remote servers is conducted without explicit manual user action.
🔒

4. System Permissions & Hardware Access

DSS Retail Studio 2026 operates as a native Windows MSIX desktop application. It requires only standard execution capabilities:

  • No Camera / Microphone Access: The application does not request, enable, or interface with any webcams, audio inputs, or recording devices.
  • No Location Services: The application does not access Windows Location APIs.
  • Local File System Access: The application only reads and writes files that you explicitly select (e.g., exporting analytical Excel reports, CSV matrices, or database backups).
🚫

5. Third-Party Services & Telemetry

DSS Retail Studio 2026 contains:

  • No Advertising SDKs: There are zero banner ads, pop-ups, or commercial advertising networks integrated into the application.
  • No Behavioral Tracking / Analytics: We do not embed telemetry trackers (such as Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or Facebook Pixel).
  • No Data Brokering: We will never sell, rent, or trade your data to third parties.
🗑️

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all data is stored locally on your machine, you have complete authority over your data retention:

  • You can reset or wipe the local database directly within the software settings.
  • Uninstalling DSS Retail Studio 2026 via Windows Settings or Control Panel removes the application and its local data cache.
👶

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

DSS Retail Studio 2026 is designed strictly for enterprise management, retail business analysis, and professional academic decision-making. We do not direct the Application to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions), nor do we knowingly solicit or collect data from minors.
